# User Experience Redesign of CieID App
> **UUXD Final Project — A.Y. 2025/2026**
> *HPHP Initiative (Help People Help People) — Client: Istituto Poligrafico e Zecca dello Stato*

## Project overview
**CieID v.2** is the final result of the redesign of the user experience of the CieID application, the official app of the Istituto Poligrafico e Zecca dello Stato that allows access to the online services of the Public Administration using the Electronic Identity Card. 

This version handles the simplification of the **PIN recovery process** and the subsequent **third-level access**, which involves **NFC scanning** of the CIE (Italian electronic identity card) specifically addressing the needs of two segments of users: **The Autonomy-Seeking Senior** and **The Trusted Digital Mentor**.

The documentation is articulated in four phases:
- PHASE A: concernes the assessment of the client's business goals, the user segmentation, the market research (through statistics, interviews and field observations) and the analysis of the client's tool and competitors.
- PHASE B: focuses on the creation of personas and scenarios based on the combination of dimentions identified in the previous phase, which will serve as a basis for the redesign.
- PHASE C: dedicated to rethinking the information architecture and the creation of low-fi wireframes through [balsamiq.com](https://balsamiq.com/).
- PHASE D: for the evaluation of the redesign versions.
